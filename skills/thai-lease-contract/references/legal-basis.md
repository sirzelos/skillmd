# Legal basis — Thai residential lease (ฐานกฎหมาย)

Updated 2026-10 (พ.ศ. 2569). Section numbers refer to the ประมวลกฎหมายแพ่งและพาณิชย์ (ป.พ.พ.) unless stated otherwise. Items marked ⚠ should be re-checked against the current official text before relying on them in a dispute.

## 1. Form, evidence, enforceability (ทำให้ใช้ในศาลได้)

| Rule | Section | Drafting consequence |
|---|---|---|
| A lease is a contract where the lessor lets the lessee use or benefit from property for a limited time, and the lessee pays rent | ม.537 | Name both roles, the property, the term, and the rent |
| A lease of immovable property **must have written evidence signed by the party to be held liable**, or it cannot be enforced by lawsuit. A lease of **more than 3 years** (or for the life of a party) must be **made in writing and registered** with an official, or it is enforceable for 3 years only | ม.538 | Keep the term ≤ 3 years, or register at the สำนักงานที่ดิน. Both parties sign. |
| Maximum term is 30 years. Longer terms are cut to 30. Renewal is possible but each renewal is ≤ 30 years | ม.540 | — |
| Witness testimony can't be used to prove the terms of a written lease against what the document says | ป.วิ.พ. ม.94 | Write **everything** into the document. Side deals that aren't written down are hard to prove. |
| An instrument without full stamp duty **can't be used as evidence in a civil case** until the duty plus surcharge is paid | ป.รัษฎากร ม.118 (surcharge ม.113–114) | Pay stamp duty at signing (§4) |
| Spousal consent is required to lease out marital property (สินสมรส) for **more than 3 years** | ม.1476 | If the term is over 3 years and the property is marital, the spouse signs a consent (หนังสือให้ความยินยอม) |
| Co-owned property: management is by majority. Leasing it out to others is safest with every co-owner signing or consenting. | ม.1358 ⚠ | List all co-owners on the deed. Get their signatures or a power of attorney. |
| Agent signing: needs a written power of attorney (stamp duty 10 baht for one act / 30 baht for several acts) | ม.798, ป.รัษฎากร บัญชีอัตราอากร ลักษณะ 7 | Attach the POA and both ID copies |
| Electronic signatures and messages are legally effective | พ.ร.บ.ว่าด้วยธุรกรรมทางอิเล็กทรอนิกส์ พ.ศ. 2544 | Notice by LINE/email can be agreed, but keep paper/registered mail as the main channel |

Witnesses: not legally required for a lease, but **2 witnesses are standard practice** and help prove signatures. Witnesses should not be parties to the contract and should be adults.

## 2. Rights & duties in the Civil Code (ม.537–571)

| Topic | Section | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Sublease / assignment | ม.544, 545 | Not allowed unless the contract permits it. The sublessee is directly liable to the lessor. |
| Delivery condition | ม.546, 548 | Lessor must deliver the property in good repair. If it is unfit for the agreed use, the lessee may terminate. |
| Contract costs | ม.547 | Fees and costs of making the lease are shared equally, unless agreed otherwise. Override in the contract if you want something different. |
| Lessor warranty (defects, eviction) | ม.549 | Rules from sale apply mutatis mutandis |
| Lessor repairs | ม.550, 551 | Lessor makes the necessary (major) repairs, except those which law or custom puts on the lessee |
| Lessee use | ม.552 | Use only for the normal or agreed purpose |
| Lessee care + minor repairs | ม.553 | Look after the property as a person of ordinary prudence would look after their own, and make minor repairs |
| Breach of use/care | ม.554 | Lessor gives notice to fix. If it isn't fixed, the lessor may terminate. |
| Inspection | ม.555 | Lessee must allow inspection at reasonable times |
| Urgent repairs | ม.556 | Lessee must allow urgent repairs by the lessor |
| Lessee must notify | ม.557 | Report damage the lessor must fix, dangers, and third-party claims to the property |
| Alterations | ม.558 | No alterations or additions without the lessor's consent. If made, the lessor can demand restoration. |
| **Rent default** | **ม.560** | If rent is paid monthly or for longer periods, the lessor must give notice and allow **at least 15 days** to pay before terminating. Parties may agree otherwise, but courts read such clauses strictly. Follow the 15 days. |
| Return condition | ม.561 | Return the property in good condition, **except normal wear and tear** (เสื่อมสภาพตามปกติ) |
| Damage liability | ม.562 | Lessee is liable for loss or damage caused by their own fault **or by people living with them** or a sublessee |
| **Limitation for lease damage claims** | **ม.563** | Claims about breach of the lease must be filed **within 6 months after the property is returned** ⚠ |
| End of term | ม.564 | A lease ends at the end of its term without notice |
| No fixed term | ม.566 | Either party gives notice of at least one rental period, but no more than 2 months is needed |
| Total destruction | ม.567 | Lease ends |
| **Sale of property** | **ม.569** | Transfer of ownership **does not end** the lease. The new owner takes over the rights and duties. |
| **Holding over** | **ม.570** | If the lessee stays on after the term and the lessor knows and doesn't object, it becomes a lease with **no fixed term**. Add "no implied renewal" and holdover-damages clauses. |
| Arrears of rent | ม.193/33 ⚠ | 5-year limitation |
| Penalty (เบี้ยปรับ) | ม.379–383 | Allowed, but the **court may reduce a disproportionate penalty** (ม.383). Keep penalties reasonable and tied to real loss. |
| Default interest | ม.224 (amended 2564) | 5% a year when nothing else is agreed (3% statutory + 2%) |
| Unfair contract terms | พ.ร.บ.ว่าด้วยข้อสัญญาที่ไม่เป็นธรรม พ.ศ. 2540 ม.4–7 | Courts can cut unfair standard-form terms and forfeiture of deposits (ม.7) |
| Guarantor | ม.680–685/1 (amended 2557) | Guarantor liability must be specific and capped. A clause making an individual guarantor a joint debtor is **void** (ม.681/1). Creditor must notify the guarantor **within 60 days** of default (ม.686). |
| Death | — (case law) | A lease is personal to the **lessee**: it ends when the lessee dies. The lessor's death does not end it; the heirs take over. Add a clause covering the occupants and belongings if the lessee dies. |

## 3. OCPB contract-control notice (ประกาศคณะกรรมการว่าด้วยสัญญา)

- **ประกาศคณะกรรมการว่าด้วยสัญญา เรื่อง ให้ธุรกิจการให้เช่าอาคารเพื่ออยู่อาศัยเป็นธุรกิจที่ควบคุมสัญญา พ.ศ. 2568** takes effect **4 Sept 2568 (2025)**. It replaces the 2561/2562 notices, which applied at 5+ units. It is issued under the พ.ร.บ.คุ้มครองผู้บริโภค พ.ศ. 2522.
- **Applies to:** businesses renting out **3 or more units** (houses, condo units, apartments) for living, counting all their buildings combined. **It does not apply** to an individual renting out 1–2 homes. Even then, use its standards as the fairness baseline.
- Key standards (mandatory if it applies, recommended otherwise):
  - Thai language, legible. Two copies, with one given to the tenant right away. Default/penalty clauses visually distinct.
  - Clearly states rent, utilities, and service fees. **No rent increase during the term.** No renewal fees.
  - Water and electricity **at the rates charged by the utility**, with unit counts shown on the bill.
  - **Deposit + advance rent ≤ 3 months' rent combined.**
  - **Deposit refunded within 7 days** of the end of the lease, with evidence for any deduction.
  - Landlord entry only with advance notice, except in an emergency.
  - No charges for normal wear and tear or force majeure. Deposit can't be forfeited without real damage.
  - Tenant may terminate early **after half the term**, with **30 days' written notice** and no arrears, without penalty (2561/2562 rule; ⚠ check the 2568 wording).
  - Landlord must not lock the unit, seize belongings, or cut off water and electricity to force payment.
- **Self-help is a crime** regardless: locking the tenant out or entering and removing belongings can be trespass (ป.อ. ม.362–365) or damage to property (ป.อ. ม.358). The lawful route is: notice → termination → **eviction lawsuit (ฟ้องขับไล่)** → judgment → enforcement officer (เจ้าพนักงานบังคับคดี).

## 4. Stamp duty (อากรแสตมป์) — needed for the contract to be used in court

- ประมวลรัษฎากร บัญชีอัตราอากรแสตมป์ **ลักษณะแห่งตราสาร 1** (lease of land, buildings, other structures, or floating houses): **1 baht per 1,000 baht or fraction thereof** of the **total rent for the whole term**. The **lessor** is the person liable.
  - Example: 12,000 × 12 months = 144,000 → **144 baht**. Deposit is not counted.
  - Pay with physical stamps cancelled by signature and date, or as cash at the Revenue Office, or by **e-Stamp Duty (อ.ส.9)** for electronic instruments, within 15 days.
  - Total rent ≥ 1,000,000 baht → must be paid **in cash** (ชำระเป็นตัวเงิน), not by stamps.
- Duplicate copy (คู่ฉบับ), ลักษณะ 28: **1 baht** if the original's duty is ≤ 5 baht, **5 baht** if it is more ⚠
- Paying late gives a surcharge (เงินเพิ่มอากร, ม.113–114). Paying at signing is cheap insurance.
- Who bears the cost can be agreed in the contract (often split, or paid by the lessor). Legal liability to the Revenue Department stays with the lessor.

## 5. Registration (lease over 3 years)

- Register at the สำนักงานที่ดิน where the land is. Fee **1% of total rent** + stamp duty **0.1%** = about **1.1%**.
- Land Office forms (ท.ด.) are used. Both parties attend or send attorneys, bringing the deed, ID cards, and house registrations.

## 6. Tax for the lessor (tell the user; not a contract term)

- Rent is income under **ม.40(5)(ก) ป.รัษฎากร**. The lessor may deduct a flat **30%** of expenses for buildings. File the half-year return **ภ.ง.ด.94** and the annual return **ภ.ง.ด.90**.
- If the **tenant is a company**, it withholds **5%** tax from rent (ภ.ง.ด.3 if the lessor is an individual) and issues a 50 ทวิ certificate. Say in the contract whether the rent figure is **before** withholding.
- ภาษีที่ดินและสิ่งปลูกสร้าง (2562): a house rented out as a residence is taxed as residential use. The owner-occupier exemption does not apply. ⚠ Check the local authority's (อปท.) assessment.

## 7. Other compliance

- **Foreign tenant:** the house owner or occupier must report the foreigner's stay to Immigration **within 24 hours** (**ตม.30**, พ.ร.บ.คนเข้าเมือง 2522 ม.38). Add a clause requiring the tenant to provide their passport and visa and to cooperate. Offer a bilingual contract where Thai prevails.
- **House registration (ทะเบียนบ้าน):** moving a name into the house register needs the house master's (เจ้าบ้าน) consent. If allowed, the contract must require the tenant to **move their name out within X days** of the lease ending.
- **Short-term letting** (daily/weekly, e.g. Airbnb) may violate the **พ.ร.บ.โรงแรม 2547**. Ban it explicitly.
- **Housing estate / condo rules:** a tenant must follow the ข้อบังคับนิติบุคคลหมู่บ้านจัดสรร/อาคารชุด. Attach the rules.
- **PDPA (พ.ร.บ.คุ้มครองข้อมูลส่วนบุคคล 2562):** state that ID copies are used only for this contract, legal compliance, and claims. Write "ใช้ประกอบสัญญาเช่าบ้านเลขที่ … เท่านั้น" across every copy.

## 8. Red flags to raise before drafting

1. Term over 3 years → registration, plus spousal consent if marital property.
2. The signer is not the deed owner → POA, inheritance documents, or co-owner consents.
3. Property is mortgaged (ติดจำนอง) → disclose it. A lease made after the mortgage may not bind the bank on foreclosure (ม.722 ⚠). Warn the tenant in the contract.
4. Deposit + advance rent > 3 months → allowed for a private individual, but courts and the OCPB see it as unfair. Recommend ≤ 3.
5. Penalties far above real loss (e.g. forfeit the whole deposit for any breach, 500 baht a day late fee on 8,000 rent) → reduce them.
6. Clauses letting the landlord lock, cut utilities, or seize belongings → remove them (criminal risk, unenforceable).
7. Rent paid in cash with no receipt → require bank transfer to the lessor's own account, or signed receipts.
8. Company tenant → withholding tax, director's signature + company seal (if their articles require it), company affidavit (หนังสือรับรองบริษัท) ≤ 3 months old.
9. Foreign tenant → TM.30, passport/visa copies, bilingual version.
10. Someone other than the tenant will live there → list all occupants. The tenant is liable for them (ม.562).
11. **House on leased land** (e.g. temple land ที่วัด/ที่ธรณีสงฆ์, Crown Property land): the lessor owns only the building. Check the land lease:
    - the holder's name
    - its end date, which must be no earlier than the house lease's end
    - whether it forbids subletting or letting the building (ม.544 by analogy). Get the land owner's or temple's written consent if needed.

    Use the `leased_land` block, so the lease ends cleanly and the deposit is refunded if the land lease ends. Attach the land lease and the latest land-rent receipt instead of a deed.
12. Requests that clash with the law or with fairness. Explain them, don't silently comply:
    - Shortening the ม.560 rent-default notice below 15 days risks the termination being held invalid.
    - Allowing the lessor to lock, cut utilities, or remove belongings risks criminal trespass (ป.อ. ม.362–365), and courts may strike it as unfair. Offer `selfhelp_silent`, which drops the promise but keeps the court route.
    - Rent in arrears combined with a 1-month deposit leaves the lessor exposed to about 2 months' loss. Say so.
